# Regimen Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 9, 2026

Regimen is a personal health tracker for iPhone and Apple Watch. It helps you log supplements, peptide schedules, workouts, food intake and lab results. This policy explains what Regimen does with your information. The short version: your data stays on your device, and the developer does not collect it.

## What Regimen stores, and where

Everything you enter is stored **only on your device**, inside Regimen's own app storage. This includes:

- Supplements, doses, dosing schedules and peptide cycle plans
- Workouts, routines, sets and rest timers
- Food, protein shake and macro logs, plus saved favorites
- Lab entries and marker values you type in
- Photos or documents you choose to attach (for example a supplement label photo or a lab result PDF). Regimen only sees the specific photo or file you pick.
- Device link status, such as whether you linked a smart scale, smartwatch or Bluetooth meter

Regimen has no online account and no server of its own. It does not upload your data to the developer or to anyone else. If you use iPhone backups (iCloud or a computer), Apple may include Regimen's app data in your backup under your own backup settings.

## Apple Health (HealthKit)

If you choose to link a watch or smart scale, Regimen asks for **read-only** access to Apple Health. It can read:

- From watches and fitness apps: steps, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability (HRV), sleep, blood oxygen and active energy
- From smart scales: weight, body fat percentage, lean body mass, BMI and waist circumference

Regimen **never writes** to Apple Health. You can turn access off at any time in the Health app or in iOS Settings. Unpairing a scale in Regimen clears its synced readings from Regimen without changing anything in Apple Health.

Health data read from Apple Health is shown to you inside the app only. **It is not used for advertising or marketing, it is not sold, and it is not shared with any third party.** It is not sent off your device by Regimen.

## Other permissions

- **Bluetooth:** used only to discover and connect to a compatible at-home blood testing meter that you choose to pair.
- **Photo library:** used only for photos you pick to attach.
- **Notifications:** used only for the local "rest is over" workout alert. These are scheduled on your device; nothing is sent from a server.
- **Face ID:** iOS handles any Face ID check on the device. Regimen never receives your face data.

## No ads, no tracking, no data sale

Regimen contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, and no third-party SDKs. The developer does not sell, rent or share your information.

## Links and exports

Some screens have links to outside websites (for example PubMed research sources or a how-to video). Those open in your browser and are covered by those sites' own policies. If you export your workouts, the file goes only where you choose to send it with the iOS share sheet.

## TestFlight beta

If you test Regimen through Apple's TestFlight, Apple collects information such as crash reports, usage data and any feedback or screenshots you submit. Apple shares that information with the developer so problems can be fixed. Apple's handling is covered by Apple's own privacy policy and the TestFlight terms. If you email feedback to the developer, the developer receives your email address and whatever you include.

## Not medical advice

Regimen is for personal tracking only. Peptide and supplement information in the app is informational and is not medical advice.
